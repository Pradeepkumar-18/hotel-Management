# UI and UX Guidelines

## Guest experience

- Put destination, dates, guests and rooms at the center of search; preserve choices when navigating.
- Display total stay price and what is included early; distinguish per-night rate from total.
- Show room capacity, bed/configuration, cancellation terms and taxes before commitment.
- Use real property imagery with useful alt text; avoid implying amenities or availability that are unverified.
- On checkout, summarize dates, room count, guest count, policies and hold expiry before payment.
- Confirmation must clearly distinguish booking confirmed from payment pending.

## Admin experience

- Organize property setup as a guided sequence: property → room types → inventory/rates → publish.
- Calendar views distinguish available, held, booked and blocked quantities and expose the date/time zone.
- Destructive or financial actions show impact, require confirmation and capture a reason.
- Lists provide filters, pagination, empty/loading/error states and keyboard-accessible controls.

## Accessibility and responsive design

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Support keyboard-only usage, screen-reader labels, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, responsive tables and touch-friendly controls. Validate date inputs and announce errors near the relevant field.
